Privacy Policy

DealSpot · Last updated: August 2026 · © 2026 Gabriel Garcia Ortiz

What DealSpot does

DealSpot shows publicly available discount/deal listings and lets you save favorites, set keyword alerts, choose your language, and open the retailer's page to purchase. Live deal data is aggregated from public feeds.

Your data stays on your device

Your watchlist, custom deals, keywords, ZIP code, theme, and settings are stored locally in your browser using local storage. They are not uploaded to our servers.

What we store (and why)

  • Push subscriptions — an anonymous device token if you enable phone notifications, so alerts can be delivered. No name or email attached.
  • Anonymous usage counts — app opens and feature taps, aggregated per day, with no personal data and no way to identify you.
  • Digest emails — your email address only if you subscribe to the weekly digest. One email a week; unsubscribe any time in the app.
  • Deal votes (🔥/💀) — anonymous counters only.

Third parties

• Paddle processes Premium payments as merchant of record; card details go to Paddle, never to us. See paddle.com for their privacy policy.
• Your ZIP code is sent only to the free public zippopotam.us API to look up your city/state.
• Tapping a deal takes you to the retailer's site, whose policies are their own.
• An automatic translation service may translate deal titles for non-English languages.

Your choices

Clear everything any time via Settings → Data → Reset everything, or by clearing your browser's site data. You may deny or revoke notification permission at any time in your browser settings.

Children

DealSpot is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
