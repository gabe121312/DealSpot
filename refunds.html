Refund Policy

💯 14-day money-back guarantee — no hoops, no hard feelings.

Who can get a refund?

If you're not happy with DealSpot Premium, tell us within 14 days of your purchase and we'll refund it in full — first purchase or renewal, no elaborate reasons needed. "I just didn't use it" is a perfectly good reason.

How to request one

Fastest: open the DealSpot app → Settings → Premium → Billing & Refunds → tap Open Paddle support (Paddle, our payment partner, processes refunds directly, usually within 1–2 business days).
Also fine: reply to your Paddle receipt email, or use the email button on the same screen — include your purchase reference (shown in the app, or in your receipt).

Timing

Refunds are returned to the original payment method, typically within 1–2 business days of approval (your bank may add a few days).

Cancellations

You can cancel your subscription at any time — you'll keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for, and you won't be charged again. Cancelling is not the same as a refund: for money back within the 14-day window, request a refund as above.

Abuse

We keep this policy generous. Repeated purchase-refund cycling may lead to declining future refunds.
